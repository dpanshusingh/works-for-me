# Ripple

A lights-out puzzle where the shape changes after every tap. Turn every light off within the tap budget (par = fewest possible taps). Each tap flips a shape of cells around it, and the shape cycles through the level's list (plus, diamond, cross, X, dot), so the order of your taps matters.

Open `ripple/index.html`, or play at https://dpanshusingh.github.io/works-for-me/ripple/
All 20 levels are solver-verified; H gives a hint, Z undoes, R resets.
